# Digital marketing: the workflow

1. **Research** (`competitor-ad-research`, marketingskills `competitor-profiling`). Who else sells this, what their ads say, which ones have run longest (longest-running = working).
2. **Strategy** (`low-budget-launch-plan`). Offer, audience, one channel first (Meta for consumer, Google for intent), daily cap, what "working" means in numbers, hard stop date.
3. **Tracking before spend.** Pixel + CAPI, UTM scheme, store attribution check. No ads until a test purchase shows up in both places.
4. **Produce** (`static-ad-maker`, `ugc-video-ad-maker`). From a `## BRIEF`, never from a mood. Product exact. Claims approved. One language per creative.
5. **Launch** (`meta-campaigns-operator`). Few cells per test (`ads.yaml`), one ABO set per cell, equal budgets, verify the split held.
6. **Decide** (`paid-ads-strategist`). On the owner's cadence, or when the gates are met. Continue, refine, new test, or kill. No content calendar.
7. **Organic and email alongside** (`social-content-research` → `b2b-social-copywriter` → `canva-post-builder`; `newsletter-system`).
8. **Report** in the house format. **Record** learnings into the owning skill.
